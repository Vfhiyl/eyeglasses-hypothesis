February 1 2 cheeff rent 20 1645 February 23 March usse due in July last 1645 2 14 Disbursements, February — March 1645/46 Delivered my cosin Harbert Jeffreys for his charges, and Thomas Ramsey with him, when they went to demaund 541i of William Haris of Bradard, in north porche of the Minster church in Heriford, due on Candlmas Day, 1645; which he failing, I must have the farme of Bradward 6 yeares longer, and the 7th years hard corn crop, & 20 parcels[?] worth of pasture; gave my cosin Harbert now for their charges in Heriford: I gave Thomas Ramsey for his paines then: I gave Timothy Pickeringe of Clifton, that was my valantine at Homcastle: To Millman of Clifton for a paier of my shuse: To Gayley, his man, for beveridge: Deliverd Thomas Walker of Tenbury, as sent from Mr [deletion] George Lea, the kings bailyf, for one wholle yeares cheeffe rent for John Pritchetts howse and the gardin to hit, in Widmarsh Streete in Heriford, due at Micklmas last past, 1645: For an aquittance for the same rent: I gave Thomas Walker, for his paines to com to Homceastle for this cheeff rent: Paid for 4 simbills[?] for 4 children: And for a rownd cake in Lent at Worcester: 291i 3s 3d tru cast Dissbursments at Homcastle & els where Sent by Isack Robins to Worcester, to pay for phisick stuff for Joyse Simons of Mrs Lucas, [a]potfiJcary: For a kay to my box thats painted with in: Gave Bes Jones, when she went to Brilley: For 2 pownd of London sope at Clifton: Gave Thomas Harris, for cariinge a leter to Mrs Anne Ailway to Worcester: I sent her also in the same letter: Gave Dick Done, for his paines to bringe my barrill of sope from Heriford to Homcastle: Paid by the Widdow Collins of Lide, 6 months interest for 801i I owe her, due July 13, 1645: Paid Mr Joshua Ailway at Worcester, for a quarter of a hundred of Bristoll sope: For 2 quiers of cap paper at Worcester: 15s 5s 4d 2s 6d 3d 18d 4d 4d ls 6d [f.] 64 9s 4d 6d 9d 6d 2s 6d 6d 3li 4s
