Som from the top: 49li 12s tru ie Aprill Heare followeth my owne dissbursments, beginning at the first of Aprill 1646, at Homcastle. 15 I gave my servant, Thomas Griffiths, at Homcastle: 4d 7 Gave Thomas Haris 6d: 6d Bes Newton Sent Dockter Johnson 20s and 10s; in all: 30s was sik Paid the Widdow Boraston for spining a wax pownd & a half of stocking yearn: 16d 17 Paid my cosin John Greenebanck for 2 basons: 3s 6d 27 Paid Goody Deakens of Clifton, for weaving 3 dozen of flaxen incle for apern strings: 6d Gave Humfrey Hardman of Nene Solers, when he brought me 12li 17s 4d rent to Homcastle: 2s 6d Paid Goodwife Button of Clifton, for 6 weecks tending Elizabeth Newton when she had an ague: 10s 28 Gave Hester Packer Packer, the butler, when she went away from dwelling at Homcastle: ls May Gave [blank] Pickering, the clarck of Cliftons church: 4d 1 Gave Shusan Wanklin, she cam to Homcastle: 6d she [sic] I delivered my cosin Jeffreys to disschard[ge] William in the Ramsey of London, tailer, for a paier of whale Covent bone bodies I had of him: 16s cae Paid the same Goody Deakens for weaving 13 yeards of flaxen incle, fyllett bredth: 4d 20 Gave my godson Henry Jeffreys, Wensday in the Whitson weeck, when he went back to Stanford: ls Paid Thomas Haris for an urinall—3d; for cariadge—4d: 7d June Gave Jack Sheriff, brought a letter from Garnons: 6d l Gave Goody Holliman of Clifton, when I dined there: 1s ae Gave ould Mrs Burraston of Clifton: 6d 6 Gave Humfrey Hardman when he brought me more rent, being 58s 6d: Is 53li 3s 5d from the very top, tru '4This date refers to the entry re Goody Holliman.
