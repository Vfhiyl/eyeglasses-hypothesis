Disbursements, July — December 1646 I begann to pay a weeckely diett for 3 prechers in Clifton, from Thursday the second day of July, 1646, unto Thursday the first day of October, being 14 weeckes, after 3s a weecke for 14 weeckes; I gave it out of my well meaning to maintaine the weeckly lecter at Clifton upon Teame: Gave Bes Jones, the dey maid at Homcastle, for well makinge the seednes cake and well feeding the braune there: Paied for the one half of 2 barrills of sturgion to eate at Homcasle: Mrs Barckley paid so much more—20s. Item—I paid for cariage from London of the one half, and her[?] paid so much too: Gave Arther Nott, the baily, for a peece of his seednes cake: Paid Joyse Simons, my maid, a quarters [wages] due at Micklmas, 1646, when she went to Worcester: Paid Stefens of Clifton for 4 paier of lamb gtoves skin gloves, at 8d a paier: Gave Humfrey Hardman, when he brought me 15li, in part of Micklmas rent of anuety from Nene Solers: Gave Mary Fareley at Homcastle: Sent ould William Calowhill, by Elizaber [deletion] Holiman, when he was sick: 14li 1s 11d tru Dissburssed at Homcastle and ells where this yeare, 1646 Paid for a new russet hatt of felt to give my cosin Harbert Jeffreys, from London: Gave Captaine Garnons ladde that brought the hatt to Homcastle: Gave Humfrey Hardman more, for bringing me more rent from Nene Solers, due at Micklmas last, 1646: Paid Humfey Hardman for to distribute to the poore of Nene Solers, for 1 yeare, due at Cristmas last, 1646: Paid Pickering, the smith, for 2 irons to putt in my smoothing iron or steelle:!*’ 42s Is 20s 5s ls 10s 2s 8d 2s 6d Is 6d 15s 6d 18d 16s
