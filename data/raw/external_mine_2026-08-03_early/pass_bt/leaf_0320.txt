Lilly, who in his youth was in the service of a salt merchant, became interested in astrology in his early thirties; he published his first almanac in 1644. He was sympathetic to Parliament and puritanism, first inferring the end of the Stuart monarchy, and then more explicitly predicting the downfall of Charles I, and overtook John Booker as the foremost parliamentary astrologer.
