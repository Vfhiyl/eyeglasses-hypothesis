xvijs. 7o1 XXXS. XS. ini iyso xijs. Charles de Liévin, Sieur de Famars (d. 1592). Liévin had been in William of Orange’s service since 1572 and had undertaken embassies to England in 1577 and 1578.
Leicester appointed him master-general of the artillery on 24 March 1586 (ARA, Eerste Afdeling, RvS 1524 [Comissieboek van der Graaf van Leycester 1586-8], fo. 38). ® Charles Francx, described on p.
