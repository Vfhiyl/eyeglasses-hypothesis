Mrs Ailway had but 250 plants Disbursements, February — March 1644/45 my god daughter, Elizabeth Geers: Paid for dawbing and mending the walles abowt William Eissams howse, when I delivired it up to Hary Eysham; the workman fownd 3 loads of clay, in all: I allowed Mathias Rufford, for divers jorneys in rideinge abowt to loke money of my dettors, but litle cam in: Gave John Pritchet, at severall tymes, to please him for watching at the gate called Widmarsh Gate: Spent at the reseight of the 4li he rece of Mr Barroll: Paid for cariing in all the mucke out of Eyshams gardin into my greate gardin; but where is the ould muck—in Fletchers garden: Paid Shusan Wanklin of Bradward, for a paier of fine woollin stockings for my self—3s; a litle yearn more—4d: Paid Mr Jenins of Leominster, the Lord Bishops rent for the half grainge land, due at Micklmas, 1644: For | yeard demy of red silk riband for shu ties: 281i 8s 4d tru cast Heryford shere dissbursments and els where For 3 wooden whistles to give children: For a yearde of ashe culler riband for a band string: For a crock to putt starch in: For a yeard of heare culler riband for my head: Paid Thomas Howlman of Clifton, for 10 elles of fine holand that a frend of his brought from Coventry, at 6s 6d an ell: Paid Joyse Simons, my maid, a quarters wages, due at St Mary Day, 1645: For a silver bodkin—2s 6d; & half an ounce of blacks silk—ls: Gave the joiner, that toke out my ould bodkin from betweene the boords in my chamber: Gave Hester Packer, the buttler at Homcastle: Gave Elinor Burraston, the coock: Gave Elyzabeth Jones, the baker & poultry keeper: Gave Isack Robins: I gave Mr William Harris of Bradward, when he cam to me at Homcastle: Paid for 400 of cabidge plants for Herifford gardin: Paid for Mathias Ruffords diett & his horse meate for 2 daies and nights: 6d 20s 9d 2s 6d 4s 3s 4d 2s 6d 6d [f£] 61 2d 6d ld 3d 3li 5s 10s 3s 6d 6d 2s ls Is Is 20s
