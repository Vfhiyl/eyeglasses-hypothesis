274 Disbursements, December 1646 — January 1646/47 Tuesday Abowt 12 a clocke at noone, Samuell Jeffreys, 8 of De- my cosin William Jeffreys 8th sonne was born, sember and baptised after the new derectory on 12 day Saturday, God bless him, midwife I gave Mrs Lewes of Tedston, the midwife: 5s I paid for 4 yeards of tinsell riband, to give my cosin Margitt Jeffreys at New Years tid[e]: 3s 4d phisick Paid Mrs Lucas of Worcester, apoticarier, for phisick stuff for Bes Newton when she had an ague at Homcastle: 19s phisick Paid for a litle phisick for my self then: 2s 6d For half an ounce of tobacco: 6d tape For a whole peece of toback tape for herelaces: Is For 2 quarts & a pinte of clarit wine: 20d For a quier of white paper—Sd; & browne—3d: 8d For a dozen of thrid poynts: . 3d 16 Mrs I sent Mrs Ailway, when she lay in at Worcester: 2s 6d Ailway Paid Mrs Alis Williams of Heriford, the yonger, Usse 1 yeares usse for 100li stock, due to her mother Mrs Willim Mrs Allis Williams, widdow, in Heriford, being due to her May 12th, 1646; she fetched it at Homeastle: 81i John Bery I delivered John Bery, my new man, at Homcastle 40s to in Cristmas, to begin his first dissburssments accownt at Bradward, the second day of January, 1646— for he went first thither to dwell;
