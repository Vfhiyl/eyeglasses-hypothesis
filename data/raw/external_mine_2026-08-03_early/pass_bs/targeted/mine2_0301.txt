256 Disbursements, August — October 1644 wages For 2 papers of bigge white pinmes—flatt heads: 8d Paid Joyse Simons, my maide, | quarters first day wages due at [deletion] Middsomer, 1644, 9 when she went to finde to Coombeies childe of Clifton: 10s Gave the 4 maids at Homcastle, at Worcester faier on our Lady Day;'*? videlicet Hester>Packer< the butler, Elinor Burraston, the coocke, Barbara [blank], the dey maide and Elyzabeth Joanes, the baker, to 10 eche—Is: 4s Gave Cherilickcom & his Jack an apes: 4d 27 Gave John Buffton of Clifton, an ould man: 3d Gave the barge man at the Forge: 3d 28 Gave a poore woman of Clifton: ld Gave Barbara, the day mayd, that lost the former Is, I gave her: 4 Is For 2 quier of browne paper—6d; & 1 quier of October white—6d: Is ] Deliverd Joyse Simonns, in part of her quarters wages wages, which was due at Micklmas, 1644: 5s 23s 1d from the top—tru July Mathias Ruffords dissbursment for the moneths of July, August, September and October, 1644, August at Heryford and Bradward & els where. Imprimis—he gave, by my apoyntment, to yong September Miles Casewall of Wickton: 5s October Paid Mrs Flecher and her mother in law howse one yeares rent for the ould howse I rent did dwell in, in Widmarsh Streete in Waees Heriford, due at Mickmas, 1644; then I left the howse: 611 Paid Thomas Bedford half a yeares wages, to clothes be due neere Alholand, 1644: 30s a shute Then he departed out of my service to his owne howse, & cloake at which tyme I gave him, to b[u]y him a shute: 4li Item—I had a firkin of metheglin from Heriford, metheglin of 7 galanes wanting a pinte, at 8d a quart; cam to it came to 18s 4d, but Maude Prichet Homeastle toke but 10s [?] and gave me the rest: 10s | Paid Mr George Lea, the kings majesties baily, for onfe] yeares cheeffe rent for John Prichets howse '8'Two fairs have been identified at Worcester: one established by letter close at the Feast of St Barnabas [11 June]; and another by presciption at the Nativity of Mary [Lady Day, 8 September].
