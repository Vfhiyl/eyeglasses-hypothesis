Gave Mr William Harris when he went to Oxford: Gave Mr Thomas Veinoll of Heruford, when he paid me 2 yeares demy tolerasion for 501i, due June 29, 1647, 10li in full, gave him: Gave Mr Nathaniell Moorley, when he brought me 10li from Mrs Cowper of Worcester, widdow: Gave my litle cosins Margret Jeffreys, Harberd, William and Edmund Jeffreys, when Humfrey Hardman Humfrey Hardman paydd me part of Nene Sollers rent for my anuety, due at Micklmas, 1647: Gave Humfrey Hardman the same tyme: I alowed Humfrey Hardman the same time for contribusion momey [money]: I alowed him, to pay Tipping for 3 contribusions: Paid my cosin Jeffreys 1 quarters diet for my self and my maid Joyse Simons, due October 19, 1647: Gave my cosin Harberd Jeffreys to pay Mr Adiss his surjon, for setting his broken legge: My cosin Harberd Jeffreys died abowt 12 a clock at noonne, when his left legge was cutt off closse under the knee on Monday, Alholand Day. He brake his legge at Clifton, 23 day, in the hole of a yeat post anunt Anthony Borastons howse, and was caried in to that howse Saturday, and was caried home to Homcastle on the morow. 18 This date refers to the entry re Elizabeth Clark.
