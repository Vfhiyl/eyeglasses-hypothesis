August Mrs Williams when my hay was all spent suger 20 day of June August 16 diet paid 20 hatt oyle 28 1644 September Disbursements, August — September 1644 Broomyeard: I paid for 2 quartes of sacke at Worcester: I gave Elizabeth Holiman of Clifton when I dined there: I gave her maide then: I dined ther one Thursday, August 8, when I was at a lecture there. Paid Mrs Aliis Williams of Heriford, widdow, for 1 yeares usse of 100li stock, due May 11, 1644: Paid to workmen for cariing som howshould for my lower howse in Heriford, to my upper new howse in the same Widmarsh Streete: Payd for hay for Mathias hors in Heriford on{e] day when he cam to towne: Gave Mr Dockter Alderns man for his horses, to fetch my coache from Garnons to Heriford: Paid Mr Henry Meredith for [blank] yearddes of tiffany, yeard broad, at 4s 8d a yeard, abating 1s; in the whole paid: Paid in Worcester for 3 pownd of loffe suger, to send my god daughter Walsh, being sick: Mathias>Rufford< spent that day with his horse in Worsester: Spent by Mr Rufford when he payed Mr Geerse 250li in Heriford, July 10, 1644: Paid at Heriford faier for a loine of mutton for Mathias Rufford & my servants there: Paid for a paier of shuse for my self: Gave Mr Tovies man, the parson of Bradwayes, that brought me a baskit of peares from his master: Paid my cosin William Jeffreys for one quarters diet for my self and Joyse Phitps Simons at Homcastle, due July the 19, 1644, the first:!
