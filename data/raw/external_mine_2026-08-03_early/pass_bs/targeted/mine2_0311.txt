I gave John>Jones< [deletion] of Cliftons Wood: For a quart of sack at Worcester: For a yeard of black tifany to putt over my eyes: For 2 yeards of fine bonlace to put on my cosin Harbert Jeffreys band at Cristmas: For 4 yeards quarter of bone lace for handkerchers, at 20d a yeard, comes to: Gave a litle boy that brought letters from Garnons from ould Mr Francis Geers, for 601i: Paid John Foord of Greate Shelsley, for 12 long ells of flaxen cloth, at 2s 8d the elle: For a quier of white paper—6d; hard wax—6d: For a litle glasse full of aniseed water: I gave John Haris of Heriford, that brought a letter from ould Mr Phillip Simons to know yf Joyse Simons weare recovered: At New Years tide, 1645, at Homcastle.
