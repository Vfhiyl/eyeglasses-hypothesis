In wittnes wherof I have hereunto sett my hand and seale dated the day and yeare first above written, provided alwayes that after discharge of the said obligation and debt aforesaid my executors be first satisfied of all arreares which at the time of my decease shalbe due to me for one anuitie or annuallment of threescore and six pounds thirteene shil- lings and foure pence to me devised by the last will and testament of my deare brother Humphry Coningsby, esquire, deceased, before any other debts be satisfied out of the premises. Joyce Jefferies, subscribed, sealed and published in the presence of Thomas Eaton, William Boraston, Joyce Symonds.
