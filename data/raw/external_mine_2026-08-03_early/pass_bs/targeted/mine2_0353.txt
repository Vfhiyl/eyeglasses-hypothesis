Joyce Jefferies, sealed, subscribed and published by the within named Joyce Jefferies, to be her last will and testament in the presence of William Berkley, Thomas Eaton, William Baraston. A codicill to be annexed to the last will and testament of me, Joyce Jefferies of Home Castle in the County of Worcester, spinster, made the third day of Aprill, 1650.
