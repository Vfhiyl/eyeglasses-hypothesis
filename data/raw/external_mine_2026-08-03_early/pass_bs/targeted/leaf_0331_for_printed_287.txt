my cosin Mary Ansley 10 2s 6d she left with J Simons to b[uly hit 16 Mr Knight 28 memirandon childe borne called Joyce Disbursements, December 1647 I sent my cosin Mary Ansley, by Mr Francis Brookes, servant to Mr William Browne of Hashfeeld, at the request of my neece, Elizabeth Browne: Gave Mr William Harris at Homcastle: Gaves James Farley at Homcastle: I paid to fill up the botle of sack which my god daughter Geers bestowed onne me; she paid 2s 6d & I paid 6d: Desember 16—gave Mr James Newtons daughter that cam to borow 40s for her father: Gave Edward Loveland, bailiff of the Teemes at Homcastle, for a peece of his seednes cake: Gave Ursula for finding the red cornelion>ring< that was my cosin Harberd Jeffreyses: < I sent Mr William Knight of Worcester, tailer, for making my black stuff gowne, by Mr Nathaniell Moorley: Paid Thomas Tailer of Worcester, a tailer, for making divers parcells of work for Mrs Elyzabeth Acton before she was maried, in full payment for all work don for her untill this present 28 day of Desember, 1647: I gave her 20li to pay for her weding clothes, so that is not to be accownted heare. I paid this 50s to hir yeong man John Harris at Homcastle & toke a note of receight of him, That my cosin, Mrs Jane Jeffreys of Homcastle, was delivered of a daughter abowt a quarter of an howre before 9 a clock at night on Thursday night, being Christmas Eves eve, and the 23 day of Desember, 1647; & hit was baptised on the Monday following, being St Johns Day, 27 day, 1647, amd and named Joyce. Ould Mrs Barckley and my self, Joyse Jeffreys, weare gossips, God blesse hitt, amen. Hit went home with Nurce Nott to the Smeeths in Greate Shelsley parish the same Munday after diner to nurce. 5li 8s 2d tru cast!”!
