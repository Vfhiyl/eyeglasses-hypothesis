Anne Paviers husband Heriford gardin M Rufford for 10li dett 901i 5s 0 this side 1645 Aprill 1 20 yong John Jeffreys christened 20 day, & born on Lowsonday, 13 day. wages Joyse S shuger 20 Disbursements, March 1644/45 — April 1645 Paid Mrs Anne Ailweis bill as followeth. For making me 2 tiffany ruffes, to Mrs Millborow Lawrance of Heriford: Paid for a pott of pomatom: For a dozen pownd of white starch: To Goodwyfe Wattis for setting cabidge plants: For 2 papers of pinnes with flatt heads: Given to sowldiers that were sent to be quartered in my new howse, 3 severall tymes: For a trace of onions to sett for seede: To John Simons for a daies worke & better in my gardin: To Charles Davies & John Simons for 3 dayes a peece in my grete gardin, at 10d a day: I alowed them, to drink, at the sowinge of the gardin: To John Simons sithence for paving the alleies and stipering up the hearbs: Paid for a capias for Mr Thomas Guillam of Tibertunne—8d; & for the serjeants feese—6d: Dissburssed and spent at Bradward the whole yeare abowt, ending on St Mary Eve, 1644: Dissbur Besides corn, mault, bacon, ruffe beeffe, piggs, pultry, sider & fruit & whitmete not receved, which cam to 50li with hay & provinder. Som totall dissburssed at Heriford, Bradward and ells where the whole yeare abowt, ending on St Mary Day Eve, 1644: New dissburssments for the whole yeare folowing, beginning the first day of Aprill 1645, at Homcastle. Imprimis—I gave Goodwyfe Lewes of Tedston, the midwife to my cosin Jeffreys wyfe of her sonne, John Jeffreys: I gave Thomas Notts wyfe of the Smeethes in Grete Shelsleys parish, that nursed my godsonn, John Jeffreys: Paid Anthony Sivall, a litle tailer, for a litle work: For a glasse of inke: Paid Joyse Simons, my maide, a quarters wages on the fore hand, to be due at Midsomer next: I sent my neece Mary Ansley, to Worcester, by T Harris: Bought of Edward Cox, by the request of Mr Joshua Ailway of Worcester, | firkin of powder shuger, weight half a hundred and 5 pownd and a half, at 12d ob the pownde: 261 6s 6d 6s 8d 3s 2d 5d 16d 6d Is 5s 2d 16d 14d 83li 7s 9d 435li 7s 9d [f. 6lv] 10s 10s 2d 3d 10s
