INTRODUCTION. 


TJOUSEWIFE'S  EXPENSE  BOOK  (formerly  "House- 
•^  wife's  Red  Book,"  revised  and  improved)  is  designed 
to  provide  a  complete  but  simple  System  of  keeping  house- 
hold accounts,  and  to  plainly  show  for  what  purchases  the 
money  goes.  Seeing  this,  the  prudent  housewife  can  alter 
her  plan  of  purchasing  so  as  to  secure  the  best  results 
with  the  least  expense. 

An  account  of  Receipts  is  as  important  as  an  account 
of  Expenses ;  so  a  daily  record  of  Receipts  for  the  whole 
year  is  provided — see  pages  4  and  5. 

Sixty  weekly  tables  of  disbursements  are  given — pages 
6  to  65  inclusive — providing  for  five  weeks  (or  parts  of  weeks) 
in  each  month.  These  tables  show  Disbursements  in 
numerous  details,  providing  for  every  usual  expense. 

The  book  is  completed  with  two  full  page  tables  showing 
the  first  and  second  half  year's  total  Disbursements  in 
detail — pages  66  and  67.  These  totals  may  be  compared 
with  the  total  Receipts  during  the  same  periods. 

The  System  plainly  shows  the  character  and  extent 
of  small  leaks  which,  agg-regate^  \LARGE  LOSSES,  and

GUIDE. 


RECEIPTS. 

1.  Enter  each  sum  received  in  the  space  provided  under  the  proper 
date  on  page  4  or  5.     At  the  end  of  each  month,  add  all  the  receipts  of 
the  month  and  enter  the  sum  under  ' '  Total. ' ' 

DISBURSEMENTS. 

2.  Enter  all  sums  spent  in  the  Week's  Table  (pag-es  6  to  65)  on  the 
proper  lines. 

3.  At  the  end  of  the  week,  add  the  sums  paid  for  each  article  and 
enter  the  total  on  the  same  line  in  the  "Total"  column.     Then  add  the 
sums  in  the  column  and  record  the  week's  total  expense  at  the  bottom. 

4.  Also  enter  at  the  bottom  of  the  Week's  Table  in  the  space  speci- 
fied, the  total  money  received  during1  the  week.      Subtract  the  week's 
total  expense  from  this  sum  (or  vice  versa )  and  enter  the  difference  in 
the  specified  place. 

5.  At  the  end  of  each  month,  from  January  1st  to  June  30th,  enter 
the  Totals  of  your  different  expenses  'in  the  proper  month's  column  in 
the  First  Half  Year's  Table,  page  66.      Then  add  the  items  and  place 
the  sum  at  the  foot  of  the  column.     This  will  show  your  total  expenses 
for  the  month. 

FIRST    HALF   YEAR. 

6.  At  the  end  of  June  add  the  amounts  on   each   respective  line  of 
the  First  Half  Year 's  Table,  and  place  the  sum  of  each  on  the  same  line 
in  the  Total  column  at  the  right — to  show  the  total  amount  each  item 
mentioned  has  cost  during  the  first  half  year. 

7.  Enter  in  the  spaces  at  the  bottom  of  this  Table  the  total  of  all 
money  received  in  the  first  six  months  ;    also   your  total  disbursements 
for  the  same  time ;   subtract  the  smaller  from  the  larger  amount  and 
record  the  difference  in  the  space  so  named. 

SECOND    HALF    YEAR. 

8.  Record  in  the  same  way,  during  the  second  half  year,   your 
monthly  expenses  in  the  Second  Half  Year's  Table,  page  67. 

9.  At  the  end  of  the  year,  add  and  record  in  the  "Total"  column 
at  the  right  of  this  table  the  amount  spent  for  each  article  during  the 
last  six  months.     Add  to  each  of  these  amounts  the  corresponding  total 
for  the  first  half  year,  and  enter  their  joint  sums  in  the   Year's  Total 
column' at  the  extreme  right  of  the  table. 

THE    YEAR'S    COMPARISON. 

10.  Finally,   at  the   end   of   the  year,  record  the  totals  of  money 
received  and  disbursed  in  the  second  half  year  exactly  as  stated  in 
paragraph  7  for  the  first  half  year.     Add  these  and  the  totals  for  the 
first  six  months  and  record  their  sums  in  the  space  provided  at  the  right 
bottom  comer   of   page   67.     Here   your    YEAR'S    TOTAL   RECEIPTS 
AND  DISBURSEMENTS  APPEAR.
