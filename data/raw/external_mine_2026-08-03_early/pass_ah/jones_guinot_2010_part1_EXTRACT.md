## Lunettes pin (confirmed)

**II §92 — Estrainnes de l’an 1433** (= 1 Jan **1434** n.s.):  
`Messire Thomas Cusac / Marzeliere / Jean de Cleux / Alain Provost` — **des lunettes d’or garnies de Besicles**.  
Jones n.558; Blanchard no.2134 cites only partial names.

## Marzelière (already promoted)

**I §20** Pierre de la Marzeliere escuyer et chambellan — Jones n.112: seigneur du Fretay; career from 1413; counselor Jean V→François II; **d.1462-08-25** (ALA B 2148; Rulon 1959).

Also I §§419, 486 (Pouancé frontier); II §§18, 92, 153, 395.

- Cusac career densified to **≥1443** living; still no obit.  
- Jean Cleux: marriage-aid + gifts; still no death.

- Accounts end ~1435 — Part II may add more but unlikely late deaths.
