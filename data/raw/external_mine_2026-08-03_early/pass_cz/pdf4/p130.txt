Cassini found that Campani’s lenses were absolutely indispensable in fulfilling his observations, and on the other hand Campani always took great pride in having been of service to the astronomer and in having had a role in the latter’s achievements. It was a period that represented indubitably the high point of astronomy in Italy and, according to many, in Europe as well.
