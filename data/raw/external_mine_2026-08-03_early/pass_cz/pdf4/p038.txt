 412 derived from records in the archives of the Vicariato di Roma, Pier Tommaso moved from one parish to another at least five times. From the parish of San Lorenzo in Damaso in 1664, in the next year he was to be found in the parish of San Salvatore in Onda, then in San Giovanni della Malva in 1670, returning once more to San Giovanni della Malva in 1683, where he remained until at least 1705, which may have been the year of his death. Records exist of more than 20 silent night clocks made by Pier Tommaso after 1664, of which some 15 have survived in whole or in part to the present time.798
