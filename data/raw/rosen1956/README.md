### Part I — what we need (and now have)

**Giordano sermon dating anchor** (section “Giordano’s Sermon”, ~pp. 25–29):

- Quotation (via Pandolfini / Laurentian MS Ashburnham): *“It is not yet twenty years since there was found the art of making eyeglasses…”*
- Continuations: new art never existed; *lettore* claimed to have seen and talked to the inventor.
- Delivery: Santa Maria Novella, Florence, Wednesday morning **29 Feb 1305** Florentine style = **23 Feb 1306** common style.
- Rosen’s inference: invention **shortly after 1286**.

Spina / Redi–Dati historiography is background only (not needed for A/B/C).

### Part II — early documentary refs (pp. 201–204)

**There is no Florence 1305 merchant will/inventory in Rosen.**

- **1305** = linguistic terminus for Chronicle *ocularia* (n.257)—**not** a probate document.
- Earliest Italian docs Rosen accepts (p.204):
  1. **1316** — *occulis de vitro cum capsula*, 6 Bolognese soldi (Albertotti)
  2. **1322** — Florentine bishop inventory (Davidsohn)
  3. **1329** — merchant theft complaint, *unum par ochialium* (Davidsohn *Forschungen* III nr.955)

Literary/art: Petrarch; Sacchetti (setting 1358); Tommaso da Modena 1352.
