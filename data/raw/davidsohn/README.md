## Entry used by this project

**Nr. 955** (p. 190) — **1329, 11 August, Florence**

- Complainant: **Ser Neri Mici da Bibbiena** (OCR: “Ser Nerins Miei ron Bibbiena”; secondary lit.: Notar aus Bibbiena)
- Forum: Official of the **Mercanzia**
- Claim: goods bought in Florence in August, robbed in **Borgo Ghiaceti** (Diacceto) while being carried to Bibbiena
- Goods include: **unum par ochialium** (one pair of spectacles), among haberdashery
- Archive cite in Davidsohn: **StAF — Mercanzia … f. 110** (OCR often “StAP”; Florence State Archives)

Cited by Rosen 1956 Part II p.204 n.278; confirms Florence as spectacle *market* by 1329 (not a 1305 will).
