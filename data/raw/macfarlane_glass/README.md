## Critical verification (2026-07-23)

**Printed pages 144–145** (Ch.8 *Spectacles and Predicaments*):

> The invention of spectacles increased the intellectual life of professional workers by fifteen years or more.

Also on the same spread: invention ~AD 1285 northern Italy; Petrarch later work; craftsmen’s active life “almost doubled”; link to printing.

**Implication for this project:** the “+15 years” sentence is **in the Chicago edition body**, not a Miller-only paraphrase. Prior “no verbatim fifteen years in body” claim is **withdrawn**.

Qualitative near-work career language elsewhere (e.g. windows “lengthened the working day”) is a **different** claim from the spectacles +15 sentence.
