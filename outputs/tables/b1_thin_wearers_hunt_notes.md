## 3) `nuno_fernandes` — Cambini export 5 June 1459

### Wear / order pin (already known)
Ilardi n.34 (AOI Estranei 222, Cambini ricordanze fol.22r), Spallanzani transcription:  
`… per chosto d'uno paio d'ochiali da cavalchare per la neve …` for **messer Nuno / Nugno Fernandi** in Portugal, **5 June 1459**. Nickname “scientist of Portugal.”

### New OA: Virginia Rau 1971 (downloaded + OCR’d)
- **PDF:** `data/raw/nuno_fernandes/rau_vanni_1971.pdf`  
  (CC BY-NC; [repositorio.ulisboa.pt bitstream](https://repositorio.ulisboa.pt/bitstreams/75e7c2e6-5370-424f-90e4-27ec113e3186/download); handle `10451/38125`)  
- **OCR:** `data/raw/nuno_fernandes/rau_vanni_1971.txt` + page excerpt `rau_p112_ochiali_excerpt.txt`

**Rau p.112 (key lines):** shipment of `um paio d’ochiali da chavalchar per la neve` destined for **Nuno Fernandes**, who **in 1460** was called **「scienziato di Portoghallo」** in Florence.  
**Rau p.114:** lists him among Bartolomeo di Iacopo di ser Vanni’s Portuguese clients as **「o scienziato Nuno Fernandes」** (no further personal dates).

### Identity / further bio
- **Not** the medieval Wikipedia “Nuno Fernandes” (12th c.).  
- **Not** João Fernandes da Silveira (ambassador; separate Cambini/Medici trail 1456–1460 in same Rau article).  
- No OA second career series (letters, offices, publications) under this name beyond the nickname attestation. Milanesi/Toscanelli Florence–Portugal cartography OA (`milanesi_toscanelli_anisig.pdf`) discusses João Fernandes / Toscanelli 1459 — **does not** identify Nuno.

### Hard-ingest?
**No.**  
- Soft bio continuity only: wear/order **1459**, nickname still current **1460** → `years_after=1`.  
- Nickname ≠ dated near-work (no letter, book, account he wrote, civic post). **Do not** add to `hard_dated_series`.  
- Useful only as identity enrichment / survival pin.
