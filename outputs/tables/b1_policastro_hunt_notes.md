## Dated events (full chrono)

### A. Pre-wear OA primary (Registro 9) — context only, not post-wear hard fuel

All at `https://www.lombardiabeniculturali.it/missive/documenti/9.{n}/`; text in `data/raw/policastro/lbc_missive_9_policastro_bundle.txt`.

1. **1452-05-03** — ducal license; already *castellano Viglevani* (9.2).  
2. **1455-01-08** (+ proroga **1455-02-01**) — leave to Milan 15 days (9.40).  
3. **1455-03-20** — standing leave license, deputy under *poena capitis* (9.44).  
4. **1459-05-10** — letter **to** him: brother *notar Thomaso* leave for June (9.180).  
5. **1459-05-30** — letter **to** castellan: Stefano da Colonia to Germany 2 months (9.185).  
6. **1460-04-02** — Easter leave from the *arx* (9.200).  
7. **1460-04-28** — Filippo da Morbegno leave without stipend stoppage (9.204).

These prove long castellan tenure **before** wear but **do not** fill 1461–1477.

### B. Wear / immediate after

8. **1460-12-12** — **FROM** Policastro to Simonetta: two pairs Florentine bone-framed *ochali*, *uno paro ne voglio per mi*. *Ex rocca Viglevani*. Ilardi n.34 (ASM Pavia cart. 757). OA quote in local Ilardi OCR.  
9. **1461-02-03** — same request **repeated** (Ilardi n.34; Covini). **Date only** in print; full text not found OA this hunt.

### C. Mid-career (target window)

10. **1467-05-28** — “lettera di nomina” of Giacomo da Policastro as castellan, per **Santoro, *Gli uffici del dominio sforzesco (1450–1500)*, Milano 1948, p. 617**, cited in LBC footnotes on **every** Registro 9 Policastro missive.  
    - **OA primary text: NOT found.** Santoro 1948 = limited Treccani/Bestetti edition (~300 copies); no IA/Hathi/Persée/Gallica fulltext located.  
    - Historiographic note: LBC editors flag that 1467 is **later** than 1452–1460 missives already calling him castellan — consistent with **reconfirmation under Galeazzo Maria** after Francesco’s death (1466), *or* with Santoro dating a specific surviving nomination leaf. Ilardi/Roveda independently say appointed **1450** and still claiming 28 years’ castellanship in **1478**. Either way, **1467-05-28 is the only mid-career day-date** recovered — and only as a secondary citation.

### D. Late / death

11. **~1478** — self-report of **45 years** Sforza service / **28 years** as Vigevano castellan (Ilardi n.35 ← Roveda 1992 p. 71). Roveda chapter **not OA**.  
12. **1478-08-26** — Simonetta diary: death, still castellan. OA primary already on disk.

**Gap:** **no OA-dated letter or castellan act by/about him for 1462–1466 or 1468–1477** (except the undetailed 1467 nomination cite).
