EYEGLASSES HYPOTHESIS — EVIDENCE SUMMARY (v3.5 tiered verdict)
============================================================

CORE COLLISION (user-facing):
  Experience-premium unlock: older scholars peak with experience; presbyopia blocked
    that window; spectacles unlocked late-career revolutionary output.
  Near-work continuation: real achievements come young; glasses only add near-work
    hours after cognitive peak — ordinary tool, not late-career revolution.
  VERDICT TILT: near-work continuation (A) over experience-premium unlock (B).

SCOPE: narrow claim (presbyopia spectacles → scholar careers → glass-center advantage).
NOT tested: full Macfarlane/Martin Glass book (vessels, telescopes, later science).

THREE-TIER VERDICT (reader-facing; see docs/VERDICT_zh.md):
  [A] NEAR-WORK CONTINUATION — ACCEPT: lowers near-work marginal cost; RCT 3/3 +
      invention history + p.190 + mass cheap trade (haberdashery-scale imports) +
      age-graded court orders (1466) + Florence occhialai density (Appendix I).
  [B] EXPERIENCE-PREMIUM UNLOCK — REJECT on adequate panels after data hunt:
      Studium/alt share45 + Wikidata P800 age-at-notable-work (n_pre≥100).
      Age-at-first/last notable work falls ~6–8y after ~1280 (opposite of B);
      trimmed 1150–1550 still ~−4.7y; glass-center vs control null (no late premium).
      Rule: insufficient → find data; adequate+wrong/null → reject. No fuzzy halt.
      22 rows inconsistent with experience-premium predictions where testable.
  [C] VIRAL / SECOND-HAND CLAIMS — DEBUNK: 'civilization started with spectacles',
      '+15 years intellectual life' — no verbatim +15y in book body; Miller review.

A true + B false + C false is coherent: more near-work hours for aging readers does
not imply late-career experience premia were systematically unlocked.

OPEN BUT NON-BLOCKING GAPS: larger named-wearer×output sample (micro n=3 so far); EMLO; optional SDBM bulk dump.

SDBM MULTI-CITY (timing_1300): Venice Place/Provenance local post/pre≈2.7/3.2 (consistent) but Bologna Place≈3.1 also rises; Paris/Oxford fall — not glass-center-specific; peaks still 1450.

ILARDI NAMED-WEARER MICRO: Petrarch/Filelfo/Lotto (high/medium + P800 dates) — ~19% of notable works at/after wear evidence (81% before); mean work age ~12y earlier than evidence age. Direction favors A over B; n=3 insufficient for standalone reject.

Modern RCT mechanism rows: 3/3 (experimental; task mismatch to medieval scholarship).
